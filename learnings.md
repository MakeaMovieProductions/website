# Learnings 

Description: These are my learnings from the projects that I worked on as a producer so far, a journal of mistakes or shortcomings that I can learn from and hopefully avoid in the future.

## Project 1: Sitting
- The callsheets on weekends are more expensive (approx 1.5 times)
- The color grading/DI process charges 2k INR per minute of footage according to the industry standard rate (Nithin's contact charges 15k INR)
- The first day of shoot is always going to be a warmup and will fetch very less footage
- SFX costs 10k INR (Nitish's contact)

## Project 2: Locked In
- Do rehearsals with actors before the shoot
- Shoot a rough version with stand-ins if you don't have a story board
- Create a shot list and create a shot "order" in which those shots can be captured with minimum wastage of time and energy
- Cover windows with trash bags to block sunlight and create night effect
- Avoid renting equipment and always opt for jugaad no matter what
